See it in a minute.

Texts, calls, photos and your screen, all on your PC.

Every feature works straight away. Turn on the optional Power mode for the extras Android normally keeps for system apps.

FeatureWorks for everyonePower mode adds
Texts See notifications and reply to SMS, RCS and chat apps from your PC. Open Google Messages for Web for full history. Nothing extra needed
Screen See your phone screen live on your PC. Tap, type and scroll with your mouse and keyboard
Hotspot One tap opens your phone's hotspot switch, and your PC joins by itself. Turn the hotspot on and off from your PC
Calls Dial from your PC, pick from your contacts, and talk through your PC's speakers and mic over Bluetooth. Recent calls list
Photos Browse your whole gallery and save any photo to your PC. Nothing extra needed
Files Drag files to your phone, and browse the folders you choose to share. Browse all of your phone's storage

Linked in about a minute.

No account to create. Your phone and PC just need to be on the same Wi-Fi.

  1. Install both apps

    Get Pixlink for Windows and the Pixlink app for your Pixel.

  2. Scan the code

    Open Pixlink on your PC. It shows a QR code. Scan it with the Pixlink app on your phone.

  3. Allow access

    Give Pixlink notification access and the permissions for calls and photos. That's it.

Your messages never leave your home network.

Pixlink talks straight from your phone to your PC over your Wi-Fi, encrypted with TLS. There's no Pixlink server, no account and no tracking. Your phone and PC check each other's security certificate every time they connect.

The code is public, so anyone can check exactly what it does. Read the source on GitHub.

Power mode, when you want more.

Android only lets system apps control the hotspot or tap the screen. Power mode uses the free, open-source Shizuku app to let Pixlink do these things too, without rooting your phone. It takes about 3 minutes to set up, once.

Read the Power mode guide
  • Control your phone with your mouse and keyboard
  • Switch the hotspot on and off from your PC
  • See your recent calls
  • Browse every folder on your phone
  • Start screen sharing from your PC without touching your phone

Questions

Is Pixlink made by Google or Microsoft?

No. Pixlink is an independent open-source project. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Google or Microsoft.

Does it work with Samsung or other Android phones?

Not right now. Pixlink is built and tested for Google Pixel phones running Android 13 or newer. Samsung phones already have Microsoft's Phone Link built in.

Does it cost anything?

No. Pixlink is free, with no ads. If it's useful to you, you can support development through GitHub Sponsors.

Why do I have to confirm screen sharing on my phone?

Android asks every time an app wants to share your screen. It's a security rule that no app can skip. With Power mode on, the prompt still appears, but your PC can open it for you.

Can I see my full text history?

Pixlink shows new messages as they arrive and lets you reply. For your full history, the Messages tab can open Google Messages for Web right inside Pixlink.
