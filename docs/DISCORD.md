# Tethr Link Discord: setup kit

Everything needed to set up the official server once someone is at a PC signed in to Discord.

## Server

- **Name:** Tethr Link
- **Icon:** `website/icon.png`
- **Template:** "For a club or community"

## Channels

**Start here**
- `#welcome`: read-only, the welcome post below
- `#rules`: read-only
- `#announcements`: read-only, new releases (follow-able announcement channel)

**Road to 100**
- `#road-to-100`: read-only, the promotion post below; the member counter goal
- `#invite-friends`: share the invite, show off your setup

**Help**
- `#setup-help`: Android, iPhone and Windows install questions
- `#bug-reports`: what happened, what you expected, phone model, Windows version
- `#ideas`: feature requests

**Chat**
- `#general`
- `#show-your-setup`: screenshots of Tethr Link on your PC

## Posts

### #welcome
> **Welcome to Tethr Link** 👋
> Tethr Link puts your phone on your Windows PC: notifications, texts, calls, photos, files, your screen and more. It's free and open source, for any Android phone (13+) and iPhone.
>
> **Get it:** https://fatalquackers.github.io/pixlink
> **Watch the reveal:** https://fatalquackers.github.io/pixlink/videos.html
>
> Need help? Ask in #setup-help. Found a bug? #bug-reports.

### #rules
> 1. Be kind. No harassment, hate or spam.
> 2. Keep it on topic in help channels.
> 3. No piracy, malware or sharing other people's personal info.
> 4. Don't post your pairing QR codes or codes from your phone.
> 5. Follow Discord's Terms of Service.

### #road-to-100
> **🚀 Road to 100**
> When this server reaches **100 members**, we'll work on putting Tethr Link in the official app stores (Google Play, Microsoft Store and the App Store), so installing it is one tap, with no unknown-apps warnings and no extra apps like Shizuku.
>
> Store rules mean a few features might change on the store versions (we'll keep everything we can and tell you exactly what's different).
>
> **Help us get there:** invite a friend who uses a phone and a PC → (invite link)
> Watch the reveal: https://fatalquackers.github.io/pixlink/videos.html
