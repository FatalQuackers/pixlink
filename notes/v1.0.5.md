**Pixlink is now Tethr Link.** Same app, new name, and it's no longer just for Pixels.

**New: every Android phone.** Tethr Link now works on any phone with Android 13 or newer: Samsung, OnePlus, Motorola, Pixel and more. iPhones work too.

**Fixes**
- Messages: texts now appear live, without reopening the tab
- The open chat scrolls to new messages as they arrive
- App icons show on notifications, chats and pop-ups again, including after restarting the PC app

## Install
- **Android:** download `TethrLink-android.apk` on your phone and open it.
- **iPhone:** install `TethrLink-iOS.ipa` with AltStore, SideStore or Sideloadly.
- **Windows (installer):** download `TethrLink-Setup-x64.exe` and run it. No admin needed.
- **Windows (portable):** download `TethrLink-windows-x64-portable.zip`, extract it anywhere and run `Pixlink.exe`.

Already have it? It updates itself.
