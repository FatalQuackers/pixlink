**New: Pixlink for iPhone.** Photos, files, clipboard, screen casting, and notifications with Answer and Decline for calls over Bluetooth. Install `Pixlink-iOS.ipa` with AltStore, SideStore or Sideloadly.

**New: hide the always-on notification.** Your Pixel can run without it and wake up whenever your PC is nearby. On the PC turn on Settings → Connections → Let my Pixel find this PC over Bluetooth, then on the phone Settings → Hide the always-on notification.

**New: What's new screen.** After every update, all apps show what changed.

**Phone**
- Find my PC: your PC rings and flashes until you find it
- Lock PC from your phone
- Home screen widget with Clipboard, Files and Find PC
- Activity: everything you sent or received, to open or copy again
- Themes: light, dark or system, plus accent colours
- Send files button on the home screen

**iPhone**
- Notifications and call alerts on your PC over Bluetooth, even with Pixlink closed
- Cast your screen to your PC
- Send photos, files and your clipboard; your PC can browse your photos
- Find my PC and Lock PC
- Reminder before a free 7-day install expires

**PC**
- Works with iPhones: pairing by QR code, iPhone notifications, screen casting
- Rings and flashes when your phone looks for it, and locks when asked
- Setting to let your Pixel find this PC over Bluetooth
- What's new screen after updating
- Updates now also arrive when a version is rebuilt

## Install
- **Pixel:** download `Pixlink-android.apk` on your phone and open it.
- **iPhone:** install `Pixlink-iOS.ipa` with AltStore, SideStore or Sideloadly.
- **Windows (installer):** download `Pixlink-Setup-x64.exe` and run it. No admin needed.
- **Windows (portable):** download `Pixlink-windows-x64-portable.zip`, extract it anywhere and run `Pixlink.exe`.

Already have Pixlink? It updates itself.
